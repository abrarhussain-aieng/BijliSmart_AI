# Typical appliance power ratings

These are typical ranges for common household appliances. They are general guidance, not official figures.
Always check the rating label on your own appliance, because real consumption depends on model, age and settings.

| Appliance | Typical power while running |
|---|---|
| Ceiling fan | 50 - 90 W |
| LED bulb | 5 - 15 W |
| Tube light (older type) | 36 - 40 W |
| Refrigerator (average running power) | 100 - 250 W |
| Split AC 1.0 ton, non-inverter | 1000 - 1300 W |
| Split AC 1.5 ton, non-inverter | 1500 - 2000 W |
| Split AC 1.5 ton, inverter | 800 - 1500 W (varies with load) |
| Electric water heater / geyser | 1500 - 3000 W |
| Electric iron | 1000 - 1500 W |
| Washing machine | 300 - 500 W |
| Water pump 0.5 HP | about 400 W |
| Water pump 1 HP | about 750 W |
| LED TV | 50 - 150 W |
| Microwave oven | 1000 - 1500 W |
| Laptop | 40 - 100 W |

Monthly energy (kWh) = (watts / 1000) x quantity x hours per day x days.
Air conditioners, water heaters, irons and pumps are usually the largest loads in a home.
